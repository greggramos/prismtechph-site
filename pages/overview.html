Company Overview

PRISM Tech PH (Principe and Ramos Information Systems Management Co.) is a full-service provider of the latest information technologies, dedicated to connecting people and organizations to innovation. Founded by Wendell Principe and Greggy Ramos, the company delivers end-to-end IT services — from software and hardware solutions to installation, networking, support, and maintenance — ensuring seamless integration across industries.

We specialize in IT Service Management that brings together cross-functional technologies, empowering businesses to operate efficiently and securely in a rapidly evolving digital landscape. By combining technical expertise with a client-first approach, PRISM Tech PH positions itself as a trusted partner for organizations seeking reliable, scalable, and future-ready IT solutions.

Vision

To be a trusted leader in IT service management, recognized for integrating cross-functional technologies and delivering seamless digital transformation.
